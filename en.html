Summer at the river, winter in the snow

Valle Maggia has two very different faces, and both are worth the trip.

Photo: swimming spot on the Maggia

☀️ Spring & summer

  • Swimming in the rock pools of the Maggia, turquoise water between white granite, the nearest spot _ minutes on foot.
  • The Foroglio waterfall and the stone hamlets of Val Bavona, off the grid and almost car-free.
  • The cable car up to Robiei, mountain lakes at 2,000 metres.
  • Walking trails from the door, from gentle to alpine.
  • Locarno, Ascona and Lake Maggiore about 30 minutes away.
Photo: chestnut woods in autumn

❄️ Autumn & winter

  • Chestnut woods turning gold, chestnuts and polenta in the local grotti.
  • Skiing at Bosco Gurin, the only German-speaking village in Ticino, about 30 minutes away.
  • Snowshoeing in the side valleys, usually with the place to yourself.
  • Fondue in the apartment, the set is there.
  • Very quiet weeks and lower prices than in summer.

Why Valle Maggia

The Ticino that carries on behind Locarno

Twenty minutes past the city it goes quiet: the same sun, the same palms by the lake, but villages of stone instead of rows of hotels.

Swimming in the Maggia
Between Bignasco and Ponte Brolla the river is a chain of rock pools of clear, cold water. The nearest is _ minutes on foot from the house.
Waterfalls and stone hamlets
The 80-metre Foroglio waterfall, the barns of Val Bavona and the grotti of Cevio Vecchio, built into the rock.
Walking from the doorstep
From an easy loop along the river to the mountain lakes of Robiei and Lago del Sambuco, it all starts in the valley.
Eating the way it has always been
Grotti serving polenta and kid goat, goat's cheese from the valley's alps, and Merlot from the Sopraceneri.

Is there parking?

Yes, free, right at the house. Coming without a car: train to Locarno, then the post bus to Cevio; the stop is about _ minutes on foot.

How do I get from Zurich to Cevio?

About 3 hours by car via the Gotthard, then Bellinzona, Locarno and into Valle Maggia. By train to Locarno and on by post bus. From Milan and Malpensa airport it is around 2 hours.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in with a key box at the house, so there is no waiting for a key. Arrival from 16:00, departure by 10:00. You get the code the day before you arrive.

Where can you swim in the Maggia?

Between Bignasco and Ponte Brolla the river is a chain of rock pools of clear, cold water. The nearest spot is _ minutes on foot. Be careful in rain: the level can rise fast, and the smooth granite slabs are slippery.

What is there to do with children in Valle Maggia?

Swimming in the Maggia, the easy walk to the Foroglio waterfall, the cable car up to Robiei, the Museo di Valmaggia in Cevio and the stone hamlets of Val Bavona. If it rains, Locarno and Ascona are 30 minutes away.

Can you ski nearby in winter?

Yes, at Bosco Gurin, about 30 minutes by car: a small, family-friendly ski area and the only German-speaking Walser village in Ticino. For snowshoeing, the tracks in the side valleys are enough.

In Valle Maggia. Half an hour beyond Locarno a valley of granite and chestnut woods begins, with pools in the river, waterfalls and villages that are genuinely quiet after 8 pm.
